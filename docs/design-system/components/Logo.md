# Logo

The Copymopy logo: a rounded blue-main square holding three white pills, for the three options every generation gives, beside the "Copymopy" wordmark in Inter Bold.

- **Lockup** (default) in app headers and anywhere the name isn't already written nearby. Use **mark** alone for app icons, avatars and favicons.
- **Size:** 28–32px in headers. The wordmark scales with the mark. At 20px and below the mark switches to a two-pill cut so it stays legible.
- **Color:** blue-main mark with grey-main wordmark on white or bg-page. On blue-main or dark grounds use `inverse` (white mark, blue pills, white wordmark). Don't recolor it otherwise, stretch it or add effects.
- **Clear space:** at least half the mark's width on every side.
- Static files (mark, inverse mark, favicon) are in the Logos asset group.
